## 💡 Interview Strategy Tips

### DO:
✅ **Lead with business value** ($390K savings, 7x faster, 50% fewer bugs)
✅ **Show you understand trade-offs** (not just a Playwright fanboy)
✅ **Be proactive about improvements** (mention gaps before asked)
✅ **Quantify everything** (numbers are memorable)
✅ **Tell stories** (real scenarios resonate)

### DON'T:
❌ Lead with technology ("I use Docker and Playwright...")
❌ Apologize for gaps ("Sorry we don't have Cloud Run...")
❌ Say "better" without measuring ("Tests are better now")
❌ Ignore business context (why does the company care?)
❌ Oversell features not implemented
